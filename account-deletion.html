Account & Data Deletion

Any school administrator can request permanent deletion of their school's ShuleTop account and all associated data — staff accounts, student records, exam results, attendance history, fee and financial records, and message logs.

This is irreversible. Because a school account holds records shared by many staff, students, and guardians, deletion is handled as a reviewed request rather than an instant, one-tap action — this protects your school from accidental or unauthorized deletion of years of records.

How to request deletion

Option 1 — from inside the app: Sign in, open the user menu (top-right avatar), and choose "Delete Account / Data." This opens this same page with your school pre-identified, ready to submit.

Option 2 — by email: Send a request from the email address registered as your school's admin account to:

privacy@shuletop.com

Please include your school's name and the admin email used to sign up, so we can verify the request.

What gets deleted

  • All staff and admin accounts belonging to the school
  • All student records, exam results, attendance, and timetable data
  • All fee, payment, and expense records
  • All message/SMS logs sent through the platform

Timeline

  1. We verify the request comes from an authorized school administrator (usually within 2 business days).
  2. We confirm the scope of deletion with you by email.
  3. Data is permanently deleted within 30 days of confirmation, and we notify you once complete.

See our full Privacy Policy for more on how we handle data before a deletion request.
