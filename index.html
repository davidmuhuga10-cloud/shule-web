School Management System for Kenyan Schools

ShuleTop helps Kenyan primary and secondary schools manage students, exams, fees, timetables and staff — and send exam results and fee reminders straight to parents by SMS, even when they have no smartphone or data bundle.

What ShuleTop does for your school

  • Student & class management — enrolment, classes, streams and promotions in one place.
  • Exams & report forms — record marks, generate grade sheets, broadsheets and printable report forms automatically.
  • SMS exam results & fee reminders — reach every parent by SMS, no smartphone or internet connection required on their end.
  • Fee & finance tracking — invoices, payments, balances and collection reports per student and per term.
  • Timetable generation — build clash-free class timetables automatically.
  • Staff & attendance management — teacher assignments, subject allocation and daily attendance.
  • Bank-grade data security — each school's data is isolated at the database level, independently security-audited.
